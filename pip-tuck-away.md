# PiP Tuck Away

A Sine mod for Zen's picture-in-picture player. Right-click the player to tuck it into a screen edge. The video keeps playing, with a narrow strip left on screen. Right-click again to bring it back.

## Using it

- Press `Ctrl+Alt+T` to tuck or restore while Zen or the player has focus.
- Click the arrow button to tuck. Right-click the button to choose an edge.
- Drag the player partly past an edge, then move the pointer away to tuck it
  there. Pull the strip back onto the screen to leave the player visible.

You can enable hover peeking in settings. Hover over the strip to see the video, then move away to tuck it again (off by default).

The player remembers its size and position, including when YouTube moves to the next video. You can also choose a fixed size, opening position, or wider strip in the [settings](docs/settings.md).

Works with [Zia](https://github.com/z1n-k/zia), too. This mod takes over its tuck controls.

## Install

You'll need Zen Browser with Sine installed. Paste this URL into Sine's mod installer:

```text
https://github.com/apoapostolov/Zen-PiP-Tuck-Away
```

Restart Zen, then open a video in picture-in-picture.
